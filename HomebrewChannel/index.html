TikTok WebOS (Fixed)

Unofficial TikTok app for LG webOS TVs. This fork loads the regular tiktok.com website because the official TikTok TV service (tv.tiktok.com) was discontinued on June 15, 2025.

HotKeys

  • 🔴 Red button — Like/Dislike
  • 🟢 Green button — Rewind five seconds
  • 🟡 Yellow button — Fast-forward five seconds
